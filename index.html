Revenue leaks before, during and after every live.

Before the live

No plan built on her own numbers

  • She picks products and deals by feel, not from data.
  • Stock is counted by hand and is often wrong.
  • She does not know her real cost after fees and postage.
During the live

Too many comments to read

  • Comments arrive faster than she can read while hosting.
  • Buyer questions go unanswered, or get a wrong answer.
  • Stock oversells because a manual count cannot keep up.
After the live

Nothing gets finished

  • Orders sit unconfirmed and unpaid.
  • Nobody follows up with the buyers who asked or reserved.
  • The same questions come back at the next live.

Usable & Verifiable Results

Proof 1

Floor price is code, not AI

Cost + packaging + postage + platform fee + minimum margin. The number is calculated in TypeScript. The agent only explains it.

Proof 2

Price Guard blocks bad deals

No discount below the floor goes through — ever. This is a plain code gate, not a prompt. It cannot be overridden by the AI.

Proof 3

Grounding Gate stops hallucination

Every buyer reply is checked against confirmed facts and live stock numbers. If it can't be grounded, it's blocked.

Proof 4

Host Monitor catches wrong claims

Listens to what the seller says on camera and compares against tonight's data. Wrong price? She sees a warning and a correction line.
